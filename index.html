Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Our Mission

Every day, millions of applicants, students, designers, and job seekers struggle with rigid government exam photo portals (such as 20KB-50KB limits, exact 2x2 inch dimensions, and clean ink signatures). Most existing tools require uploading sensitive personal documents to unknown remote servers or paywalling basic exports.

ResizeHub solves this through zero-upload client-side engineering. By taking full advantage of modern HTML5 Canvas APIs, typed binary arrays, Web Workers, and jsPDF, all operations happen directly on your device with lightning speed, zero bandwidth waste, and absolute confidentiality.
