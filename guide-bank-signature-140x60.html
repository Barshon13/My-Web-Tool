Bank Job Signature: 140×60 px Under 20 KB

Guide • Updated: 2026-11-11 • Free, in your browser — your photos are never uploaded

Bank job forms — IBPS Clerk and PO, SBI Clerk and PO, and other public-sector bank recruitments — all ask for a scanned signature, and the size is strict: 140×60 px, under 20 KB. Get it wrong and the application form will not accept your upload.

This guide shows you how to make a clean, accepted bank signature in a few minutes, using only a pen, white paper and your phone.

Quick facts — bank job signatures (IBPS, SBI)

  • Size: 140×60 px or 300×80 px
  • File size: 10 KB – 20 KB (strictly under 20 KB)
  • Resolution: 200 DPI
  • Paper: pure white paper, dark black or dark blue ink
  • Quality: high contrast, no shadows, no grain

Step 1: Sign small, on white paper

Bank signatures are small and wide — that is why the size is 140×60 px, much wider than it is tall. Sign on plain white paper with a dark black or dark blue pen, and keep the signature compact, about the width of two fingers. Do not sign big and then shrink it a lot; a signature drawn at roughly the right size stays sharp.

Step 2: Scan or photograph in good light

Place the paper flat in bright, even daylight and photograph it from directly above, or use a scanner if you have one. The ink should look black against clean white paper. Shadows, yellow light or a tilted camera all make the signature harder to read — and harder to compress under 20 KB.

Step 3: Crop tight, then resize to 140×60 px

Crop away all the empty paper so the signature fills the frame. Then open ResizeHub’s Signature Resizer, upload the cropped image and set the size to 140×60 px. If your notification asks for 300×80 px instead, use that size — both are standard bank-form sizes, so follow your own form.

Step 4: Compress under 20 KB and check it

Set the file-size target to 20 KB and download the result as a JPG. Before you upload it to the bank form, open the file and zoom in: the signature should be dark and readable, with a clean white background. A good bank signature usually lands between 10 KB and 20 KB.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Signing with a light or coloured pen — light ink fades when compressed and can become unreadable.
  • Using ruled notebook paper — the lines show through and add noise to the file.
  • Uploading a photo of the whole page — crop first, or the file will be far over 20 KB.
  • Capital letters only, or a different signature from your ID — use the signature you normally use on official documents.
  • Reusing an old, blurry signature — if your old file is already compressed, make a fresh one from a clean scan.
