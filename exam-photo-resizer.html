How to use it

Exam application portals reject photos that are the wrong size or over the file-size limit, and every exam sets its own numbers. This tool resizes your photo to the size your exam asks for and compresses it to a file size you choose, all in your browser. Your photo is never uploaded to a server. The exam photo size limits guide lists the common limits side by side if you want to compare them before you start.

  1. Open the Exam Photo Resizer and upload a recent, clear, front-facing photo of yourself.
  2. Set the size your exam notification asks for. The default is 200x230 px, which is the size IBPS asks for.
  3. Pick a target file size: 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB, matching the range in your exam notification.
  4. Check the preview to make sure your face is clear and the photo is not stretched or squashed.
  5. Download the photo as a JPG. It stays on your device — nothing was uploaded at any point.

When to use it

Use this tool whenever an exam form asks for a photograph within a specific size and file-size range:

  • IBPS applications: resize your photo to 200x230 px and pick a 20 KB or 50 KB target, inside the 20-50 KB range IBPS asks for.
  • NEET (UG) and JEE Main 2026: both ask for a JPG photo of 10-200 KB. Pick a target inside that range — 50 KB or 100 KB works for either.
  • GATE 2026: GATE accepts a JPG of 5-600 KB at 200x260 to 530x690 px, a wider range than most exams, so set the size and target your notification states.
  • UPSC applications: UPSC asks for a JPG photo of 20-200 KB. Always check your own notification first, because limits change between cycles.

Frequently asked questions

What size photo does IBPS need?

IBPS asks for a photo of 200x230 pixels and 20-50 KB. This tool defaults to 200x230 px, and you can pick a 20 KB or 50 KB target to stay inside that range.

What is the photo size for NEET and JEE?

NEET (UG) 2026 and JEE Main 2026 both ask for a JPG photo of 10-200 KB. Pick a target file size inside that range, such as 50 KB or 100 KB, and download the JPG.

Why does the portal reject my photo?

The usual reasons are a photo that is the wrong pixel size or a file that is larger than the limit in your exam notification. Resize the photo to the size the notification states and pick a target file size inside its range, then try uploading the new JPG.

Can one photo work for two exams?

Only if both exams accept the same size and file-size range. Check each notification before reusing a photo, because limits change between cycles and differ between exams. If the sizes or ranges do not match, make a separate resized copy for each exam.
