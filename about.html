About ResizeHub

Built in Bangladesh, for students and job applicants.

ResizeHub is a free, client-side photo tool built by Barshon Hossain, a B.Sc. Mathematics student in Jashore, Bangladesh.

It makes passport photos (600×600 px, 2×2 inch), signatures under 20 KB (300×80 px or 140×60 px), exam photos to exact KB limits (20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB), batch resizing, compression, and image-to-PDF — all in the browser, so your photos never leave your device.

It was built for students and job applicants in Bangladesh who need photos that match SSC, bank, passport, and visa form requirements exactly, without paying a studio or uploading sensitive documents to an unknown server.

How it works

  • Open a tool, drop in your photo.
  • Pick the exact size your form asks for.
  • Download the result. Nothing is uploaded.
