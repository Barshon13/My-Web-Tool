Contact & Support

We reply from Bangladesh (Asia/Dhaka).

Questions, bug reports, or a photo size we should add? Email us at mailp6962@gmail.com.

Please do not send photos containing personal documents by email — describe the size you need instead (for example, "SSC signature, 300×80 px, under 20 KB").

Before you write

  • Tell us which tool you used (for example, Signature Resizer or Passport Photo Maker).
  • Tell us your browser and what happened — what you expected, and what you saw instead.
  • If a form rejected your photo, tell us the exact size and KB limit it asked for.
