Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026 • Last updated: 2026-11-10

Short version: Your photos never leave your device. ResizeHub processes everything locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or share your photos.

1. What we collect from your photos: nothing

All resizing, cropping, compression, conversion, and PDF work happens locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos, signatures, exam cards, and documents are never uploaded to our servers, never stored by us, and never shared with anyone. When you close the tab, the browser clears the temporary data.

2. Accounts and logins

ResizeHub does not run accounts or logins. We do not ask for your name, address, or documents to use the tools.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to see aggregate, non-personally-identifiable usage — for example, page views and which tools are used most — so we can improve the site. Analytics may set cookies in your browser. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting image processing.

4. Advertising and cookies

ResizeHub does not currently show Google AdSense ads. If advertising is added in the future, this policy will be updated before ads are turned on. Third-party vendors, including Google, may then use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site or other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com and at aboutads.info/choices.

5. Children's privacy

ResizeHub is a general utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
